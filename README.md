# Threat Quotient

### AI-Powered Continuous Cyber Risk Quantification & Investment Optimization Platform

> **Turn Cyber Risk Into Business Decisions.**

Threat Quotient is a cybersecurity risk intelligence platform designed to continuously translate technical security telemetry into **financially quantified cyber risk**, identify the factors driving that risk, simulate mitigation scenarios, and optimize cybersecurity investments.

The platform connects the chain:

**Security Telemetry → Vulnerabilities → Control Effectiveness → Incident Likelihood → Financial Impact → Enterprise Risk → Mitigation → Investment Optimization → Executive Decision**

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Traditional cybersecurity risk programs often rely on qualitative ratings such as:

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

While useful for prioritization, these ratings do not directly answer the questions executives and security leaders need to make investment decisions:

- How much financial exposure does our current cyber risk represent?
- Which assets and services contribute most to that exposure?
- What is driving the risk?
- What happens if a critical vulnerability remains unresolved?
- How much risk reduction would MFA, EDR, PAM, segmentation, or patching provide?
- Which security investments provide the greatest modeled risk reduction for a fixed budget?
- How does cybersecurity risk translate into business and financial exposure?
- How does our current security posture map to major cybersecurity frameworks?

Threat Quotient is designed to answer these questions through a unified cyber-risk quantification and decision-support workflow.

---

# 🎯 Core Objectives

Threat Quotient aims to:

1. Continuously quantify cybersecurity risk in monetary terms.
2. Correlate security telemetry with business asset criticality.
3. Estimate incident likelihood and financial impact.
4. Identify the primary drivers of enterprise cyber risk.
5. Simulate cybersecurity scenarios and control improvements.
6. Calculate Expected Annual Loss and tail-risk metrics.
7. Optimize cybersecurity investments under budget constraints.
8. Provide executive and technical risk dashboards.
9. Map security controls to major cybersecurity frameworks.
10. Provide an AI-powered interface for querying and explaining modeled risk.
11. Support simulated security telemetry and event ingestion.
12. Maintain backend interfaces that can later connect to real enterprise security platforms.

# 🏦 Demonstration Environment

The prototype uses a fictional financial-services organization:

**NovaBank Financial Services**

The demonstration environment contains synthetic enterprise data representing:

- Business units
- Critical business services
- Enterprise assets
- Vulnerabilities
- Security controls
- Security incidents
- Threat activity
- Asset dependencies
- Financial impact assumptions
- Mitigation actions
- Compliance requirements

All demonstration data and financial risk values are **synthetic and modeled**.

They should not be interpreted as real-world financial, security, regulatory, or loss estimates.
